# Mission Schema

The format the Conductor emits. Every mission is a quest with requirements and a reward.

## Top-3 mission (full detail)

```
### N. [MAIN|SIDE] <title>  ·  score <0.0>
- **Why now:** <the radar trigger: what just shipped or changed>
- **Why you:** <tie to an active project, focus area, or your bottleneck>
- **Potential:** <grounded reward: a real number or time-save. If not grounded, mark it an estimate>
- **Requires:** <prereqs: cost, access, time, who must say yes>
- **Effort:** low|medium|high  ·  **Confidence:** high|medium|low  ·  **Source:** <url>
```

## Rest-of-board mission (one paragraph)

```
### N. [MAIN|SIDE] <title>  ·  score <0.0>
<2-3 sentences: what it is, why it matters to you, the candidate action, effort. Src: <url>>
```

## Rules
- MAIN quest moves your top metric or serves an active project. SIDE quest is capability or time-save.
- Anti-horoscope bar: concrete why-now + why-you (active focus) + grounded potential (or marked estimate), else it does not ship.
- Daily push capped at top 3. The rest live in the board.
- Carry forward a dismissed section so the Conductor learns and stops re-pitching rejected categories.
